## Is P0 credible?
**Partly.** The frozen replay-v2 meets validation v2 on 15 Apr: GEH 87.2%, free-flow speed error 14.7%, congestion-state agreement 92.2%, no stray wrong-lane teleport.

**What it gets right:**
- Ordinary Wednesdays run at 96–97% GEH with no congestion.
- The closure phases come from TD's lane readings.
- Counts are within 2–3% at TDS90024/29/33.
- Drivers switching to Route 3 at Ting Kau match TD's Tai Lam Tunnel detectors.

**What it gets wrong:**

- One of three seeds fails validation, on a single wrong-lane teleport.

## Does a Ripple plan beat P0?
**Not under our rule** (win in every cell):
- With vehicle-actuated signals at Sham Tseng, P2 is 3–5% better in almost every cell. Seed 2028 at 50% compliance is the exception: +0.7% with no minutes charged outside.
- With fixed-time signals, P2 loses at 70% compliance, by +6.5% to +16%.

**What is robust:** blanket diversion (P1) is worse than doing nothing in all 45 cell × outside-minute cases, by +1.8% to +69%. The targeted P2 is better than P1 in all 45.

## Data's reading
- **Tuen Mun does not give a defensible saving against doing nothing.** The win depends on how much green TD gives the Sham Tseng exit slip, which we can't source, and on compliance staying moderate.
- **It does support a defensible refusal-and-targeting story:** Ripple would advise against "send everyone round" and keep any advice to a small share.
- That matches the Aberdeen pitch, with a second incident behind it. Whether that is worth adding before the claims freeze is the Lead's decision.
- Aberdeen is untouched.
