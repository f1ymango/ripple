## 2. The model (`sim/tuen-mun/`, `scripts/tuen_mun/`)

**Network.**
- OpenStreetMap for Yau Kom Tau to Siu Lam, including Castle Peak Rd and the Ting Kau / Route 3 interchange.
- TD's signed speed limits.
- The crash site cut out of the 5.4 km edge.
- Signals: 36 in all. Their positions are OSM's; Sham Tseng Interchange is TD junction NT405. Their timings are unknown.
  - netconvert's fixed guess gave the Sham Tseng Interchange 12 s of a 90 s cycle per approach and gridlocked Castle Peak Rd on an ordinary day.
  - The model therefore uses vehicle-actuated control.
  - It has to be checked against TD's ordinary-day segment speeds before any plan run.

**Loops.** 27 TD detectors, TDS90017–43, placed 2–32 m from TD's coordinates. Counts are withheld where a detector disagrees with its neighbours on ordinary days (`scripts/tuen_mun/detectors.py`).

**Demand.**
- TDS90019 on the day.
- The Ting Kau merge as on ordinary Wednesdays.
- Castle Peak Rd from 2024 census stations 6209 and 5657.
- Exit shares at Sham Tseng and Siu Lam fitted on ordinary Wednesdays.
- Siu Lam drivers end at Siu Lam.

**P0's own diversion.**
- At Ting Kau, E's `drivers_leave` sends drivers who see the queue onto Route 3, with the share fitted.
- The Tsuen Wan-side shortfall is added as Castle Peak Rd traffic.

**Known disagreements between sources** (they limit what the model can match):
- TDS90034–37 read 10–20% below TDS90029–33, with no junction between except the bus-only Siu Lam bus-bus interchange.
- TDS90039–43 read 20% below TDS90033, where the census suggests a 5% drop.
