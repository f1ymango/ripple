# Component C follow-up: why the first P0 cannot support plan comparison

This is a bounded diagnosis of IN-26-03365 on 7 October HKT. It compares
three runs on the **same PR #46 network, demand, loops, observations, seed,
07:25–10:35 horizon, no speed signs and default vehicle-type driver settings**:
the [agreed-for-diagnostic P0](p0-diagnostic-2026-10-07.md), a **timing
sensitivity** that moves only the assumed all-lane phase from 09:36 to 09:15,
and a no-closure control. The 09:15 choice is motivated by AID01104's sustained
zero-flow bins. It is **not** an observed physical transition and cannot be
silently substituted for TD's 09:36 report.

Generated evidence: [same-bin comparison](../results/twin/oct07-p0-followup-comparison.json),
[09:15 evaluation](../results/twin/oct07-p0-all-0915-evaluation.json),
[09:15 queue audit](../results/twin/oct07-p0-all-0915-diagnostic.json),
[09:15 run summary](../results/twin/oct07-p0-all-0915-run-summary.json), and
[no-closure control](../results/twin/oct07-full-no-closure-control.json).
The comparison script checked all **607 eligible observed count pairs** for
exact agreement before comparing the simulations. Every run reached 10:35;
the two incident runs had an external 180-second wall guard. The no-closure
control used Data's `run_flow_balance` 180-second timeout.

| Same-input run | GEH < 5 / 607 bins | Speed MAPE | Waiting at 10:35 | Teleports |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No closure | 90.12% | 56.90% / 790 bins | 964 | 0 |
| P0, all lanes assumed 09:36 | 87.15% | 47.33% / 790 | 840 | 72 |
| Sensitivity, all lanes assumed 09:15 | **83.86%** | 41.84% / 789 | 648 | **130** |

The no-sign speed errors do not inherit the 15.8% speed-sign baseline result.
The 09:15 variant lacks one scoreable speed bin; every count pair is identical.
Its 1,849 unfinished vehicles and 130 teleports make its apparent speed-score
improvement unsuitable as evidence of credible queue dynamics.

## 1. Early near-zero flow and upstream queue

For the six eligible AID01104 bins at 09:05–09:30, the detector counted
**17** vehicles. The original partial-lane P0 passed **547**, essentially
the no-closure control's **545**. AID01103 upstream counted **280** against
**826** in either run; its observed speed was 6.1–13.5 km/h while the
simulation was about 46–47 km/h. Closing only slow lane `_0` at 08:55
therefore does not produce the observed early restriction or upstream queue.

Moving the assumed all-lane phase to **09:15** makes AID01104's zero-count
bins at 09:15, 09:20 and 09:25 match (09:30 observed 2, simulated 0), and
cuts the six-bin total to **184**. It still misses AID01104's 09:05 and 09:10
counts, and AID01103 has **403** against 280 across the six bins. The
sampled upstream slow-vehicle proxy grows sooner but its all-lane peak rises
from 303 to **321**. The new timing reduces overall GEH and increases the jam
teleports from 63 to 108. **Timing matters, but this single change does not
resolve the incident.** No sourced capacity or lane-change rule for the
remaining fast lane is available, so one has not been invented.

## 2. Traffic downstream of the reported full closure

At AID01106 during the four eligible 09:40–09:55 bins, observations show
**310** vehicles at about 42.6–45.7 km/h. P0 with the 09:36 full closure
produces **59**; the 09:15 sensitivity produces **42**. The no-closure
control produces **418**. The count evidence lies between these cases while
the observed downstream traffic is still moving, so the reported closure
does not imply zero traffic at every later detector.

The PR #46 route builder already includes two **ordinary-day inferred joins
after edge `44419212` and before AID01106**, each 62.5 veh/h: feeder
`1198059128` is Wong Chuk Hang Road and `26560290#0` is Ocean Park Road in
the rebuilt OSM network. The model can thus register nonzero AID01106 flow
while the flyover edge is closed. Those ordinary-day allocations plus vehicles
already downstream do **not** reproduce 310 observed vehicles in 20 minutes.
This is a missing or misallocated incident-window source/route question,
not permission to inject 251 invented vehicles. AID01105 between the two
detectors has no usable lane scope in the current audit, making localisation
harder. The [OSM placement audit](../results/twin/osm-network-audit.md) also
puts AID01106 at **0.10 m from an edge start**, marks it as an endpoint case,
and gives a 20.73 m lane-0 lateral offset. Its physical cross-section needs
review before treating the 310 observed vehicles as a precisely located
downstream boundary. Data should check original AID01106 lane readings and whether traffic
could reach it from those named roads during the event; Simulation should
check their physical connection and placement with a reviewed map.

## 3. Ramp queue and network priority

The demand builder's ramp flow `f22` starts on edge `46766432` and schedules
2,481 vehicles over the run. The P0 FCD contains contiguous IDs `f22.0` to
`f22.1640`: **1,641 inserted**, leaving **840**, equal to the total pending
departures. The 09:15 sensitivity has 1,833 inserted and **648** pending;
earlier full closure frees some downstream space but does not fix the ramp.
FCD samples cannot alone certify each outside-network vehicle's origin, but
this is strong corroboration of the ramp bottleneck. Even the **no-closure**
control ends with **964 waiting** and zero teleports. AID01109 over the
available window has 4,879 observed versus 3,633 original P0, 3,643 early
variant, and 3,620 no-closure simulated counts.

The compiled network's ramp signal `11030048926` has 80 seconds green, 5
yellow and 5 red. At the downstream merge into `1194711693`, ramp edge
`485146396` has minor-priority `m` connections while mainline
`462275986#1` has major-priority `M`. This gives a concrete queue mechanism
even with a long green. Actual ramp signal timing and right-of-way have not
been established from a primary road source. Changing green time, priority
or demand just to improve GEH would be an unsupported network intervention.

## 4. Reopening and decision

After the assumed 10:02 reopening, AID01104 counts **405 observed** across
four eligible 10:05–10:30 bins, versus **643** original P0, **852** early
variant and **385** with no closure. The timing sensitivity makes the release
surge worse; upstream slow-vehicle samples clear by about 10:20 in both
incident runs, but teleports and unfinished vehicles confound that recovery.
The closure timing and downstream access issue must be settled before calling
that clearance realistic.

## Reproduce the timing sensitivity

From a fresh checkout of this branch, install the dependencies and SUMO 1.27.1,
then extract the [P0 input packet](../results/twin/oct07-p0-replay-inputs.zip)
once. Run the checked-in [09:15 config](../sim/scenarios/p0-0915-sensitivity.json)
with an external 180-second wall guard:

```powershell
Expand-Archive results/twin/oct07-p0-replay-inputs.zip -DestinationPath .
python -m src.twin.run_scenario --config sim/scenarios/p0-0915-sensitivity.json --label p0-0915
```

The packet's loop definitions write their XML output under
`sim/work/p0-replay/results`. Use a fresh extraction or separate checkout for
each replay so those outputs cannot be confused. The checked-in sensitivity
config changes only the all-lane phase offset and output locations from the
packet P0 config; it makes no claim that 09:15 was physically observed.

**Decision:** no tested P0 variant validates closure flow, upstream queue,
downstream flow and recovery together. Do not use either incident run to
rank P1/P2. Keep the simplified model available for a narrowed comparison;
it has its own validation gate. The next source-backed actions are:

1. **Data/E:** verify AID01104 lane-by-lane readings, AID01103 congestion
   onset, AID01106 flow and validity through 10:35, and whether the named
   downstream feeders have observed counts or other provenance. Specify the
   unspecified-lane policy separately from retrospective physical replay.
2. **Simulation:** review the two feeder connections, AID01105 placement,
   ramp merge priority and signal against actual road evidence. Keep 09:15
   only as a labelled sensitivity. A new run should change one supported
   assumption at a time and retain all pending/teleport diagnostics.
3. **Lead:** decide whether new physical evidence warrants another bounded
   SUMO P0, or whether Week 3 plan comparison must use a narrower fallback
   claim. No response-plan metric is certified by these runs.
