# Ripple project status

## The 2026 alpha

Ripple began as **UST Ripple (Team 1075)**, an entry in the Hong Kong
Inter-University GenAI Hackathon for the SDGs 2026, Track 1: Green Transport &
Smart Mobility. This repository preserves the competition prototype, final
pitch materials, source code, saved results, and the working notes that explain
how the team reached them.

The [judge-facing tag](https://github.com/f1ymango/ripple/tree/judge-facing-2026-10-18)
is the fixed reference for the competition-era repository. The final
[PowerPoint and demo media](deck/README.md) are kept in
`deck/deliverables/final/`. The [two-case demo pack](results/demo-oct16/)
contains the saved outputs behind the pitch. Dated notes in `docs/` are a
historical record; they can describe earlier plans or deadlines.

The alpha is a research and demonstration prototype. Its console replays
archived incident data; plan comparisons are simulated. It does not control
traffic infrastructure or deliver live instructions to drivers. Read the
[README](README.md#what-the-alpha-prototype-shows) and
[two-incident evidence pack](docs/demo-two-incidents-2026-10-16.md) for the
specific claims and their limits.

## Continuing Ripple

**Ripple is the initiative and name; this implementation is its 2026 alpha.**
Future work may extend this code or start again with a different architecture.
Either path can live in this repository without rewriting the competition
record. Keep the tagged snapshot and final submission artifacts intact, and
document substantial new work as a new phase with its own claims and evidence.

There is no commitment that the alpha's internal module contracts or
dependencies will be carried into a future version. Before developing or
deploying a new phase, review its data rights, dependency security, model and
simulation validity, and the intended operator workflow anew.
